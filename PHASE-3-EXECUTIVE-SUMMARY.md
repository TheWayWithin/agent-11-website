## Key Recommendations

### 1. Homepage Attribution
**Use Option 3**: Footer of hero section
- Least intrusive to conversion flow
- Maximum credibility without disrupting CTA
- Subtle enough for first-time visitors, visible enough for curious users

### 2. Ecosystem Launch Strategy
**Start with AGENT-11 + 2-3 active projects**
- Credibility comes from shipping, not planning
- Too many "coming soon" projects can dilute trust
- Better to add projects as they launch

**Suggested Initial Lineup**:
- AGENT-11 (production-ready) ✅
- LLM.txt Mastery (if active development) ✅
- AI Impact Scanner (if planning phase has substance) ✅
- Hold Evolve-7 and Solo Market until further development ⏸️

### 3. Professional Photo
**Style Guide**:
- Natural lighting, casual professional attire
- Approachable expression (slight smile, direct eye contact)
- Simple background (office, outdoor, neutral wall)
- Think "technical founder" not "corporate executive"
- Minimum 800×800px resolution

### 4. External Links
**Wait for confirmation before linking**:
- Don't deploy with 404 links
- Option 1: Link to "Coming Soon" landing pages with email capture
- Option 2: Remove external links, keep descriptions internal
- Option 3: Link to GitHub repos if projects are open-source
