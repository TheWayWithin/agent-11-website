## 6. Visual Design Recommendations

### Color Palette
- **Free Tier Card**: White background, blue accents (trustworthy, primary CTA)
- **Support Card**: Warm gradient (gold/yellow tints) - inviting, grateful, NOT aggressive
- **CTA Buttons**:
  - Primary (blue): Action-oriented (Deploy, Get Started)
  - Secondary (gold): Supportive (Buy Coffee)
  - Tertiary (gray): Social (GitHub star)

### Icons & Imagery
- Coffee cup (☕) for Buy Me a Coffee sections
- Star (⭐) for GitHub actions
- Checkmarks (✅) for feature lists
- Heart (❤️) for community/appreciation messaging
- NO DOLLAR SIGNS in support section (avoid transactional feeling)

### Card Layout
- **2-Column Grid** on desktop (50/50 split or 60/40 free vs support)
- **Stacked Cards** on mobile (Free tier first, Support second)
- **Equal Visual Weight** - Support card should NOT look like "premium tier"
- **Soft Shadows** - Friendly, approachable (not corporate)

## 7. Messaging Strategy & Voice

### Tone Attributes
- **Authentic**: Real founder talking to real founders
- **Grateful**: Appreciative, not entitled
- **Community-Focused**: "We" not "I" in support messaging
- **Transparent**: Honest about what support funds
- **No Guilt**: Support is optional, not required

### Language to Avoid
- ❌ "Upgrade" (implies free version is lesser)
- ❌ "Premium" (creates hierarchy)
- ❌ "Unlock" (implies withheld features)
- ❌ "Limited time" (creates false urgency)
- ❌ "Donate" (sounds like charity, not appreciation)

### Language to Use
- ✅ "Support" (collaborative, not transactional)
- ✅ "Appreciate" (gratitude-based)
- ✅ "Community-driven" (collective effort)
- ✅ "Optional" (removes pressure)
- ✅ "Help fund" (transparent about usage)

## 8. Conversion Psychology Applied

### Reciprocity Principle
- Give everything free first (trigger reciprocity instinct)
- After users experience value, suggest support
- Frame as "pay it back" not "pay it forward"

### Social Proof Integration
```
💬 "AGENT-11 saved me 87.5% rework on my MVP. Supporting Jamie was a no-brainer - this framework is worth 100x more than a coffee." - [Founder Name]
```
**Placement**: Above or below "Why Supporting Helps" section

### Loss Aversion (Inverted)
- Emphasize what you DON'T lose by supporting (nothing - still 100% free)
- "Your contribution doesn't unlock features - AGENT-11 already gave you everything"

### Commitment & Consistency
- Start with easy ask (GitHub star = 5 seconds)
- Build to medium ask (Share on Twitter = 30 seconds)
- End with value ask (Buy Coffee = $5-10 one-time)
